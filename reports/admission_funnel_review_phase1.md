## 1. Methodology & Data Sources

**Data Sources Used:**
* `aa_journal` (Opportunity Evaluations)
* `blocked_signals` (Admission Rejections)
* Recovered Ledger: `opportunity_funnel.csv`

**Row Counts Analyzed:**
* Raw Opportunity Loops: ~79,000
* Valid Journal Evaluations: 2,866
* Blocked Signals: 2,674
* Executed Trades: 192

**Methodology:**
Data was aggregated to map the percentage drop-off at each stage of the funnel. Text analysis was performed on the `block_reason` column in the `blocked_signals` table to identify the primary causes of admission rejection and the affected symbols.

## 2. Opportunity Funnel Mapping

The funnel exhibits extreme attrition, converting only ~0.24% of raw opportunities into executed trades.

| Funnel Stage | Count | Survival % (Stage) | Survival % (Total) | Attrition Point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Raw Opportunities** | 79,000+ | 100.0% | 100.0% | Starting Baseline |
| **2. Evaluated Signals** | 2,866 | ~3.6% | ~3.6% | ~96.4% of raw loops generate no formal evaluation (likely scout mode discards). |
| **3. Blocked Signals** | 2,674 | - | - | 93.3% of formally evaluated signals are vetoed before execution. |
| **4. Executed Trades** | 192 | 6.7% | 0.24% | Only 6.7% of evaluated signals survive the governance and risk gates. |

**Observation 1:** The primary organizational bottleneck is not opportunity generation, but the admission and risk gates. The system evaluates plenty of setups but structurally rejects 93% of them.

## 3. Admission Gate Analysis

The 2,674 blocked signals are not randomly distributed. They cluster around specific rejection types and symbols.

### Most Common Rejection Causes (Frequency Ranked)
1. **Admission Score Too Low (Dynamic Threshold Failure):** The overwhelming majority of blocks occur because the signal scores (e.g., 83/85 or 63/85) fall short of the dynamically elevated admission thresholds.
2. **Negative Paper Performance Penalties:** Signals are structurally penalized (raising the admission threshold) due to recent poor performance in that specific symbol.
3. **Asset Class Specific Headwinds:** Metals specifically trigger a "Metals require higher confirmation and reduced size" structural block.
4. **Portfolio Concentration:** E.g., "Metals concentration gate: active metals count 1 at limit 1."
5. **Tick Path Quality:** "Tick path is choppy, not a clean intraday push."
6. **Velocity Limits:** "Symbol activity cap active: 2 entries in 60 minutes."

### Affected Symbols
* **XAGUSD (Silver):** The most frequently blocked symbol by a massive margin.
* **XAUUSD (Gold):** Second most frequently blocked.
* **NZDUSD / AUDUSD:** Frequently blocked due to poor recent paper performance.

**Observation 2:** The admission gates are highly reflexive. Because XAGUSD and XAUUSD had negative early paper performance, their admission thresholds were raised (e.g., to 85). This causes subsequent XAG/XAU signals to be blocked because they score an 83, creating a feedback loop where the symbol is locked out of trading.

**Observation 3:** Metals (Gold/Silver) face compounding vetoes. A single XAGUSD setup might simultaneously fail the 85-point threshold, face a negative performance penalty, face a generic metals penalty, and hit a portfolio concentration limit.

## 4. Evidence Quality Assessment

### Data Limitations
* **Concatenated Block Reasons:** The `block_reason` column in the database stores multiple veto reasons as a single semi-colon delimited string (e.g., "Trade admission score 83/85 is too low.; Recent XAGUSD paper performance is slightly negative."). This makes automated programmatic parsing difficult without string splitting.

### Unknowns
* **Counterfactual Performance:** We know 2,674 signals were blocked, but the `opportunity_funnel.csv` does not tell us whether those signals would have been profitable or unprofitable. We do not know if the gates are saving capital or leaving money on the table.

### Next Questions Generated by Evidence
1. **Are the dynamic thresholds too strict?** If a trade scores 83/85 and is blocked, what is the historical expectancy of 80+ scoring trades?
2. **Is the "Metals" penalty justified?** Are metals structurally harder to trade, or did they just have a bad random sequence early in the paper trading phase?

### Area for Future Review
* **Phase 2 (Risk Blocks) / Simulated Outcomes:** We must analyze the `opportunity_funnel_simulated.csv` generated by the Shadow Risk Engine. If the simulated outcomes of the 2,674 blocked signals show positive expectancy, the admission gates are too strict. If they show negative expectancy, the admission gates are functioning perfectly.

## 5. Summary Hypotheses

*Hypotheses are presented for future contradiction testing only.*

* **Hypothesis 1:** The dynamic threshold system creates an "accidental lockout" where a short string of early losses permanently raises a symbol's threshold so high that it can never execute again, preventing mean reversion.
* **Hypothesis 2:** The current governance gates are successfully filtering out low-quality trades, and the 6.7% execution rate represents a healthy, highly selective trading posture.
