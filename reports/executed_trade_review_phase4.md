## 2. Survivor Profile

Opportunities that survive the funnel and execute share the following characteristics:

* **Score Ranges:** The average admission score of an executed trade is **87.52**. The absolute minimum score was 70.0 (the dynamic threshold baseline for Forex).
* **Duration:** They are overwhelmingly short-term trades. 72% of tracked executions are closed within 60 minutes.
* **Side:** They exhibit a ~60/40 structural skew toward the short side (Sells).
* **Asset Class:** Despite strict portfolio limits on metals, XAGUSD and XAUUSD still account for nearly 75% of all executed trades.

---

## 3. Admission Success Review

Comparing the executed cohort against the previously reviewed blocked cohort:

* **Scores:** The blocked population clustered around scores in the 60s and 70s. The admitted population averages ~88. The threshold is actively enforcing a stark separation in score quality.
* **Symbol Representation:** XAGUSD dominates *both* the blocked list and the executed list. The extreme number of blocked XAGUSD signals is partially a function of the sheer volume of XAGUSD opportunities generated by the engine.

## 4. Opportunity Flow Review

The overall opportunity funnel from generation to execution:

1. **Opportunity Generation (Raw Loops):** ~79,000+
2. **Evaluation (Journal Entries):** 2,866 (3.6% survival from raw loops)
3. **Governance Blockade (Admission/Risk/Portfolio):** 2,674 vetoed.
4. **Execution:** ~148-192 executed. (6.7% survival from evaluation).

**Overall Survival Rate:** Only ~0.2% of raw opportunity loops result in a live trade. The system is extremely selective by design.

---

## 5. Anomaly Watchlist Update

* **XAGUSD (Silver):** *Strengthened rationale for monitoring.* Silver is not just the most blocked asset; it is also the most traded asset, accounting for half the executed book. It is the core driver of the organization's current activity. 
* **XAUUSD (Gold):** *Strengthened rationale for monitoring.* Follows the exact same pattern as Silver (high velocity, high block rate, high execution rate). 

**Observation 1:** The trading engine currently functions primarily as a precious metals mean-reversion/breakout engine that occasionally trades forex, rather than a diversified macro engine.
