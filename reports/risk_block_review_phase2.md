## 1. Block Inventory

This phase reviews the cohort of opportunities that generated a valid trade idea but were blocked by the Admission Gate or Risk limits.

* **Total Blocked Opportunities Reviewed:** 350 simulated records (372 total DB entries).
* **Side Distribution:** Strongly skewed towards short ideas.
  * Sell: 234
  * Buy: 138
* **Symbol Distribution (Top 2):** Extreme concentration in metals.
  * XAGUSD: 227
  * XAUUSD: 65
* **Block Reason Distribution:**
  The vast majority of blocks are triggered by dynamic admission threshold failures (e.g., scoring 83 against a dynamically elevated requirement of 85). These threshold penalties are compounding, often triggering simultaneously with portfolio concentration limits and symbol velocity caps.

---

## 2. Simulated Outcome Review

The Shadow Risk Engine generated counterfactual pathways for the 350 blocked signals to answer what *would* have happened had they been allowed to execute.

* **Outcome Distribution:**
  * **Hit Stop Loss:** 227 instances (64.9%)
  * **Hit Take Profit:** 75 instances (21.4%)
  * **Open / Timeout:** 48 instances (13.7%)
* **Excursion Metrics:**
  * **Average Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE):** +3.48 price units
  * **Average Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE):** -5.25 price units

**Observation 1:** The mathematical expectation of the blocked signal cohort is heavily negative. The trades gravitate toward their stops (-5.25 MAE) much faster and further than they move toward their targets (+3.48 MFE).

---

## 3. Block Quality Assessment

The core research question is whether the attrition generated by the Risk and Admission gates is protective or suppressive.

* **Clearly Protective Blocks (64.9%):** 
  The 227 signals that were simulated to hit their stop loss represent pure protected capital. The gates successfully identified and discarded structurally losing trades.
* **Clearly Suppressive Blocks (21.4%):** 
  The 75 signals that hit their simulated take profit represent the "false negative" rate. The gates suppressed profitable trades.
* **Ambiguous Blocks (13.7%):** 
  The 48 signals that timed out or remained open represent sideways chop where capital would have been tied up without generating alpha.

**Observation 2:** The Risk and Admission blocks are functioning primarily as a highly effective, protective filter. The 21% false negative rate is an acceptable opportunity cost to achieve a 65% defensive win rate against drawdowns. 

---

## 4. Evidence Quality Assessment

The observations above are derived from synthetic offline simulation. The following limitations apply to the evidence:

* **Missing Risk Brackets:** Because the signals were blocked at the Admission Gate, the AA decision engine never progressed to formulating trade-specific Stop Loss and Take Profit levels. 
* **Simulation Assumptions:** To bridge the dependency gap, the Shadow Risk Engine assumed fixed mathematical brackets (0.5% Stop Loss / 1.0% Take Profit) based on AA's default scaffolding parameters.
* **Data Limitations (Execution):** The simulation assumes a perfect fill at the arrival tick price. It cannot simulate counterfactual slippage, spread widening, or latency. If slippage were included, the 21.4% "suppressive" win rate would likely decrease further.
* **Unknowns:** It is unknown if AA would have managed the open trades dynamically (e.g., cutting losses early) before they hit the hard mathematical brackets.
