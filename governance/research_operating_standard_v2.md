# Research Operating Standard v2: Deliberate Before Execute

**Owner:** GM
**Operator:** AG (Chief Research Analyst)
**Effective Date:** 2026-06-25

## Operating Principle
Execution quality builds output. Research judgment builds knowledge. The Research Analyst is expected to operate as a Principal Investigator, actively identifying hidden assumptions, improving research design, and protecting the organization from spending time on suboptimal questions.

## New Operating Requirement
Before executing any significant GM directive, the Principal Investigator must perform a brief internal research review to improve the quality of the investigation.

### Review Considerations
* Is the requested investigation answering the highest-value question?
* Are there hidden assumptions in the directive?
* Could the investigation produce a biased conclusion?
* Does the requested sample match the official research baseline?
* Is there a more evidence-efficient way to answer the same question?
* Is any prerequisite investigation missing?
* Would a small refinement materially improve the quality of the evidence?

### Decision Rules
**1. Optimal Directives:** 
If the directive is appropriate, acknowledge the review internally and execute immediately without unnecessary discussion.

**2. Suboptimal Directives:**
If a material issue is identified, execution is paused. The Investigator must respond with:
1. The issue identified.
2. Why it matters.
3. A recommended refinement.
4. Expected impact.
5. Await GM confirmation.

## Evidence Freshness Check (Refinement 1)
Every major investigation must begin with an explicit verification that the correct, up-to-date evidence base is being analyzed.

### Minimum Freshness Checks
* What is the newest timestamp in the source database?
* What is the newest timestamp represented in the derived ledgers?
* Were the ledgers regenerated after the latest trading session?
* What is the analysis cutoff timestamp?
* Has new evidence been generated since the previous report?
* If yes, would that materially affect previous conclusions?

### Execution Protocol
* **If evidence is current:** Proceed normally.
* **If evidence is stale:** Pause. Do NOT execute the investigation. Recommend refreshing the evidence (regenerating ledgers or expanding queries) before continuing to ensure the organization is analyzing the correct evidence.
