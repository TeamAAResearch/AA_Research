## What We Believe Today

**Confirmed:**
* AA is running.
* AA is evaluating markets.
* AA is generating signals.
* AA is rejecting many opportunities.
* Metals currently dominate observed activity.

**Not Yet Proven:**
* Whether the admission threshold is too high.

## Primary Research Question
Do not ask: *"Why is AA rejecting trades?"*  
Ask: *"Where do FX opportunities disappear?"*

An FX opportunity can disappear at:
1. Signal generation
2. Governance
3. Portfolio controls
4. Risk controls
5. Admission scoring
6. Execution

The objective is to identify the dominant bottleneck.
