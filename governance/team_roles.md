# AA Team Roles

Date created: 2026-06-24
Scope: Research governance only

## Operating Principle

Current Mission:

Determine where FX opportunities disappear across the trading pipeline and whether AA can generate sufficient high-quality FX flow to support a profitable trading business.

The objective is understanding.

The objective is not optimization.

The team exists to explain AA's trading business, not to optimize it prematurely.

During Data Collection & Learning:

- Observation is not hypothesis.
- Hypothesis is not finding.
- Finding requires surviving contradiction.
- No recommendations are generated unless explicitly requested by GM.
- Repository contents take precedence over memory, assumptions, prior conversations, and agent recollection.

## Reporting Cadence

Daily:

- Theo reports system health.
- Iris reports feed and market coverage issues if present.
- Ledger updates continue if evidence is available.

At N=25 / N=50 / N=100 FX opportunities:

- Full opportunity economics review.
- Funnel, attrition, session, pair, side, and blocked-opportunity analysis.

Weekly / GM Review:

- Ari, Rowan, Nolan, Clara, and Mira explain AA's trading business.
- Mason, Vera, Iris, Theo, Evelyn, Sofia, and Helena provide supporting evidence and governance checks.

## Decision Boundaries

The team may classify evidence.

The team may identify unanswered questions.

The team may prepare analyses.

The team must not recommend changes unless explicitly requested by GM.

Evidence alone does not authorize change.

Recommendations require explicit GM request regardless of evidence strength.

Changes require evidence that:

- The pattern is persistent.
- Contradictory evidence has been checked.
- The impact on opportunity economics or data integrity is clear.
- The recommendation is explicitly requested.
